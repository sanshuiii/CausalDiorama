
## Additional rules for this run (motion of the environment and of added things, background replacement)

- Motion the instruction asks for in the environment (waves rolling in, branches, flowers or palm fronds swaying, snow or rain falling, mist or smoke drifting, clouds moving, light flickering or shifting, birds crossing the distance) is part of the edit. Write it as ongoing motion into background.appearance, into text.scene and into every text.timeline interval (what moves, where and how, all through the clip), so that it reaches the video model's caption.
- Do not model environment elements that should move (water surfaces, foliage, a sky with moving clouds, falling snow, mist, flames) as backdrop rectangles or static_props: static geometry tells the video model that those regions stay still. Keep backdrops and static props for rigid scenery only (walls, rocks, buildings, furniture, the ground).
- A thing the instruction adds that is described as animated (flapping or gliding wings, a fluttering tail, scarf or flag, a crawling insect, a turning head, ruffling feathers) keeps that motion: give it self_moving: true and a root_track along the described path when it travels (keep its position fixed when it is described as tracked to a surface or standing), and write its own movement into its appearance and into every timeline interval.
- When the instruction replaces the background, every entity of plan A that belongs to the old background (scenery behind or around the subjects such as boats, piers, trees, buildings, parked vehicles, furniture, passers-by) is removed in plan B (list it in edit.removed) unless the instruction keeps it. Keep only the subjects the instruction names or that are clearly the foreground subjects; the new background comes from background.appearance and the environment.
